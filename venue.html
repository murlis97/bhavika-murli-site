Getting There & Parking यात्रा और पार्किंग

By Air

San Diego International Airport (SAN) is the closest major airport, about 40 minutes from the venue. McClellan-Palomar Airport (CLD) in Carlsbad is a smaller, closer alternative for private flights.

By Car

From I-5 or SR-78, follow signs toward Vista Ridge Lane. Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is readily available throughout the area — expect longer wait times during peak event hours.

Parking

Complimentary self-parking is available in the venue's main lot. A shuttle runs from the overflow lot on Vista Ridge Lane every 15 minutes starting an hour before each event.

While You're in Town

Things to Do Nearby आसपास घूमने की जगहें

LEGOLAND California

A family-friendly theme park in nearby Carlsbad, about 20 minutes from the venue — a fun stop if you're traveling with kids.

Oceanside Pier & Beach

One of the longest wooden piers on the West Coast, with beachfront restaurants and shops, roughly 15 minutes away.

Guajome Regional Park

A quiet county park with walking trails, a lake, and picnic areas — a nice, easy morning outing.

Rancho Buena Vista Adobe

A historic 1850s adobe estate and gardens right in Vista, worth a stroll if you have an afternoon free.

Moonlight Beach, Encinitas

A popular local beach about 25 minutes south, with soft sand and easy parking — great for a sunset walk.

Vista's Antique Gas & Steam Engine Museum

A quirky, well-loved local museum a few minutes from the venue — fun for something a little different.
