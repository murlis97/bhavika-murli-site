Haldi

Tuesday, November 24 · 2:00 PM · 29314 Twin Oaks Valley Rd, Vista, CA

An intimate, joyful ceremony where family applies turmeric paste to the couple as a blessing for health and radiance ahead of the wedding. It's playful, a little messy, and close-knit — mostly family and close friends. Expect music, laughter, and turmeric that gets everywhere.

Suggested Attire

Comfortable, casual Indian wear in yellow, mustard, or marigold-orange tones. Wear something you don't mind staining — turmeric doesn't wash out easily. Flat, comfortable footwear recommended.

Sangeet Night

Tuesday, November 24 · 6:00 PM · 29314 Twin Oaks Valley Rd, Vista, CA

This is a full-on party — dinner, performances, a DJ, and dancing well into the night. Come with energy to spare; there will very likely be a dance floor moment expected of you.

Suggested Attire

Vibrant, party-ready Indo-western or fusion wear — think sequins, shimmer, and jewel tones like fuchsia, royal blue, or emerald. Prioritize something you can actually dance in: skip floor-length gowns or delicate sarees that restrict movement, and choose flats or block heels over stilettos.

Baraat & Wedding Ceremony

Wednesday, November 25 · 2:00 PM · 29314 Twin Oaks Valley Rd, Vista, CA

The main event. The groom arrives in a festive baraat procession with music and dancing, followed by the traditional wedding ceremony and rites, and the reception celebration afterward.

Suggested Attire

Traditional, formal Indian attire — lehenga, saree, or Anarkali for women; sherwani, bandhgala, or kurta with churidar for men. Rich, festive colors are welcome (red, maroon, gold, emerald, royal blue); traditionally, all-white or all-black are avoided at Hindu ceremonies. The baraat involves some walking and dancing outdoors, so comfortable shoes are worth it.
