Ganesh Sthapna & Tel Ceremony - Bride's side ritual

Tuesday, November 24 · 8:00 AM

A vital pre-wedding ritual in Hindu culture where the bride's family officially accepts and blesses the groom.

Attire

Indian wear - Kurta, Anarkali, Lehenga.

Ganesh Pooja & Vansh Ropan - Groom's side ritual

Tuesday, November 24 · 9:00 AM

A sacred pre-wedding ritual, honoring Lord Ganesha, blessing the family line, and preparing the couple for a joyful and obstacle-free union.

Attire

Indian wear - Kurta, Anarkali, Lehenga

Haldi

Tuesday, November 24 · 2:00 PM

An intimate, joyful ceremony where family applies turmeric paste to the couple as a blessing for health and radiance ahead of the wedding. It's playful, a little messy, and close-knit — mostly family and close friends. Expect music, laughter, and turmeric that gets everywhere.

Attire

Comfortable, casual Indian wear; something you don't mind staining.

Sangeet

Tuesday, November 24 · 6:30 PM

This is a full-on party — dinner, performances, a DJ, and dancing well into the night. Come with energy to spare; there will very likely be a dance floor moment expected of you.

Attire

Vibrant, party-ready Indo-western or fusion wear — think bling, sequins, shimmer, and jewel tones.

Baraat & Wedding Ceremony

Wednesday, November 25 · 1:00 PM

The main event. The groom arrives in a festive baraat procession with music and dancing, followed by the traditional wedding ceremony and rites, and the reception celebration afterward.

Attire

Traditional, formal Indian attire; rich, festive colors are welcome; traditionally, all-white or all-black are avoided at Hindu ceremonies..
